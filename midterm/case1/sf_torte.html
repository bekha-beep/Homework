Salad Bar

by Muminovich

A classic Russian salad made with potatoes, carrots, eggs, ham, pickles, green peas, and mayonnaise. This delicious and creamy salad is perfect for holidays (6 servings).

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes - boiled and diced
  • 2 carrots - boiled and diced
  • 4 eggs - hard-boiled and diced
  • 8 ounces cooked ham diced
  • 3 pickles - diced
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion (optional)

Directions

  1. Wash the potatoes and carrots.
  2. Boil the potatoes and carrots until tender. Let them cool.
  3. Boil the eggs until hard-boiled. Let them cool and remove the shells.
  4. Peel and dice the potatoes and carrots into small cubes.
  5. Dice the eggs, cooked ham, and pickles into small pieces.
  6. Place the potatoes, carrots, eggs, ham, pickles, and green peas in a large bowl.
  7. Add mayonnaise, salt, and black pepper.
  8. Mix all the ingredients together until well combined.
  9. Refrigerate the salad for at least 1 hour before serving.
  10. Serve chilled and enjoy!
